# Project identification marks

These SVG marks were retained from the existing Frameworks prototype. Their upstream locations and any recorded fallback are listed in `sources.json`. They identify benchmark participants and do not imply affiliation with or endorsement of Frameworks. The site applies a light monochrome treatment in dark mode and a dark treatment in light mode, including hovered and selected marks. jte uses a contrast-preserving filter so its embedded background does not erase the lettering. Original assets are retained unchanged except for the documented TanStack favicon adaptation, which removes its rectangular background for a legible monochrome mark.

Hover colors are sourced alongside each project in `catalog.json` and
`feature-projects.json`. Multicolor marks use one documented accent (Marko's
green and Astro's pink); SolidStart/SvelteKit share their parent palette.
Monochrome marks use the theme foreground, including Expo/Exact, Next.js,
Remix and TanStack. Askama, Sailfish, jte, Handlebars and Topcoat retain their
monochrome treatment instead of an invented brand hue. Rocker's Java fallback
mark also stays neutral rather than assigning Java's palette to Rocker.

Feature-row hover shades preserve the source hue and adjust toward black or
white only when necessary to maintain 4.5:1 text contrast on the hover surface.
The logo, name and total share that shade. Support-bar colors continue to mean
built-in, official package and third-party package; they do not change by tool.

Collection license notices are retained in `/licenses/devicon.txt` (MIT) and
`/licenses/simple-icons.txt` (CC0). Individual project names and marks remain
subject to their owners’ trademark and brand policies.
